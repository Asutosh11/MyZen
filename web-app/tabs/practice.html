DAILY MEDITATION

🧘 The Main Daily Practice

When sitting for your main daily session, follow these 4 simple steps to quiet the mind:

1
Prepare the Body
Do some exercise, stretching or movement. Make it a timetable everyday. So that your body can sit upright and still without restlessness and fatigue. When beginning a session, find a comfortable place, on a chair or on the floor with a mat, and sit straight.
2
Observe Natural Breath
Sit comfortably, close your eyes, and watch your breath flow naturally without forcing it. At first the breath will be fast paced, which will slow down in a few mins. When mind drifts away from thoughts, bring attention back to breath as soon as you notice
3
Anchor Focus Inward
After a few mins, breath will slow down, its not racing anymore. After few more mins, breath will be felt at the nose and then at the point between the eyebrows. When breath becomes very very slow, forget the breath and just focus & feel the point at the space between your eyebrows (Kūṭastha). Gently return focus here if thoughts wander.
4
Rest in Stillness
Remain aware. If mind gets drifted away with thoughts, bring the attention back to the point between the eyebrows as soon as you notice. When ending the session, open the eyes gently. Be in the same position for a few moment, feel the changes, before getting up.
