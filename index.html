🧘 The Main Daily Practice

When sitting for your main daily session, follow these 4 simple steps to quiet the mind:

1
Prepare the Body
Do light stretching or movement first so your body can sit upright and still without restlessness.
2
Observe Natural Breath
Sit comfortably, close your eyes, and watch your breath flow naturally without forcing it.
3
Anchor Focus Inward
Feel the breath sensation at the space between your eyebrows (Kūṭastha). Gently return focus here if thoughts wander.
4
Rest in Stillness
Effortlessly remain aware. Let thoughts pass like clouds—rest as the calm observer.

💡 What are Patanjali's 8 Limbs & How do they measure meditation?

What are the 8 Limbs? Patanjali's 8 Limbs (Ashtanga) are eight interconnected branches of internal yoga—ranging from external ethics (Yama/Niyama), steady posture (Asana), and breath refining (Pranayama), to sensory withdrawal (Pratyahara), single-pointed focus (Dharana), effortlessness (Dhyana), and pure absorption (Samadhi). They grow together like branches of a single living tree.

How they measure effectiveness: While clock duration tracks physical effort, Patanjali's 8 Limbs serve as an internal ruler for quality. By logging both Time (duration in minutes) and Highest Limb Touched (depth 1–8), you measure both time length and the far more vital dimension: true internal depth of attention. In every session, the depth achieved means far more than time spent.

🌐 What & How The Web App Works

MyZen is a minimalist meditation journal that measures depth, not just duration. The web app acts as your live personal reflection dashboard. Anyone can view the overall depth philosophy, but when you log in, it becomes your private interactive meditation journal.

⚡ GitHub Sync & Data Ownership

Your entries are saved to sessions.json in your own Github repository. The Login process creates a public Github repo in your own account, if it doesn't exist. Else uses existing. By signing in with a Personal Access Token, updates commit straight to your own GitHub via the REST API - no middleman servers.
